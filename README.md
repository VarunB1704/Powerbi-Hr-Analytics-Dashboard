# 👥 HR Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

## 🔍 Overview
This Power BI HR Analytics Dashboard provides insights into employee attrition, workforce demographics, and job satisfaction to help HR teams make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered
- What is the overall employee attrition rate?
- Which departments and job roles have the highest attrition?
- How does attrition vary by age, gender, education, and salary slab?
- What is the relationship between job satisfaction and attrition?
- How does work experience impact employee turnover?

## 📊 Key Metrics
- Total Employees
- Attrition Count & Attrition Rate
- Average Age of Employees
- Average Salary
- Total Working Years

## 📈 Key Features
- Attrition analysis by Gender, Age, Education, Salary Slab, and Job Role
- Job satisfaction analysis by role
- Interactive slicers for department selection
- Clean KPI cards for quick insights
- Business-focused layout for HR stakeholders

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- DAX
- Excel (sample HR dataset)
